      Javelin 1.7

      Javelin 1.7 is a massive release that spans several years of work and tens of thousands of lines of code.

      Download now from:

      • Git-Hub
      • Steam

      Join the community!

      Major changes

      • Adventurers-guild
      • New dungeon-inhabitant: Hunter

      Beta 22

      • Minigames
        • Warlock-Tower. Wave-based. Highly risky and highly rewarding gauntlets.
        • Arena redesign with evolving teams.
        • Catacombs. 4 progressing classic-horror dungeons.
      • New consumables: bombs and vaporizers

      Beta 20

      • Rune-gear and crafting runes
      • Dungeons
        • Branches
        • World-portals
        • Specialized chests
        • Decoration
        • Lore
      • Identify consumable items

      Beta 19

      • Haunt redesign and new haunts (14 in total)
      • Attacking now uses a full attacks-sequence

      Beta 18

      • New items: Staves, Rods and Flasks
      • New world-locations: Wilderness

      Beta 16

      • Quests and diplomacy for towns

      Beta 6

      • Support mouse
      • Minigames: Arena; Deep Dungeon
      • Redesign city-management with 7 traits and 36 projects
      • Casting rings
      • Martial discipline: Steel-Serpent

      Minor changes

      • Varying number of foes in encounter-tables
      • Towns
        • Develop more quickly in the early game
        • A lodge per town
      • Volume-option
      • Icons for unexplored dungeons
      • Smaller squads start with experience
      • Concede Arena-matches
      • New battle-maps
      • Radial vision
      • List kit-upgrades in kit-guide
      • Larger text

      Beta 22

      • World
        • Contested territories
        • Location-colors indicate their challenge-tier
      • Towns
        • Semifixed items in the starting shop
        • Redesigned quests and diplomacy: 3 quest-categories; 7 quest-types and 6 mandates
      • New dungeon-features: altars and corrupted altars
      • 9 new combat-maps focusing on shores and town-shores
      • New spells: Detect Magic, Read Magic, Word of power: Kill, Flare Burst and Overland Flight.

      Beta 20

      • Dungeons
        • New features: passages; maps; crates; recipes and paths
        • Hidden traps and chests in decorations
      • Towns
        • Redesigned diplomacy and quests
        • Redesigned mines
        • Rune-shops
      • Separate screens for using items and equipping gear
      • Camping can be used to quickly heal with spells

      Beta 18

      • Major eidolons recharge; minor eidolons are consumed
      • Improved-placing battle-units

      Beta 16

      • New dungeon features: Herbs and Camp-fires
      • New combat maps
        • Shores
        • Constructed Underground
        • Dynamic maps for town-sieges
      • New Questions-guide
      • Detect and prompt when-all battle-units are automated
      • More details provided in crash-reports
      • New art

      Beta 15

      • Skills
        • Bluff
        • Sense Motive
        • Are now either trained or untrained
      • Dungeons
        • Names
        • Encounters-tables
        • Inhabitants: leaders; prisoners; brokers
        • Autoreveal features with Knowledge
        • Temples now have varying number of floors
      • Sell items on shops or drop them from the inventory

      Beta 14

      • Elite monsters and foes with kits
      • Dungeons
        • Rebalanced
        • Doors and keys
        • Different visuals per tier

      Beta 13

      • Redesigned Arena
      • Redesigned Dungeons and Temples
        • Template-based system for generating maps
        • 4 tier-sizes
      • Redesigned incursions
      • Constriction damage when grappling

      Beta 6

      • New locations
        • Natural resources
        • Assassins-guild
        • 6 haunts
      • Infiltrate hostile locations
      • Unrevealed world
      • 5 new kits
      • Autoattack by pressing tab
